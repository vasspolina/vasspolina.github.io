Musician and new media artist originally from Moscow with Vietnamese roots.

Steeped in a rigorous classical piano background, she shifted her focus to the theremin, embracing a direct, almost tactile encounter with sound that breaks free from traditional constraints.

Anya invites audiences into a realm where technology, personal history, and the human body converge in immersive sonic and visual experiences
