About.

Liminal space is a dedicated stairwell and common‑area cleaning service for residential buildings across Berlin. We look after the shared thresholds of daily life — entrances, landings, banisters, lifts, basement corridors, and courtyards — on a fixed weekly schedule.

In stairwells (wooden Altbau treads or stone landings), steps are swept and wet‑mopped from the top floor down to the street door. In hallways and handrails, brass fittings, glass panels, letterboxes, and intercoms are wiped clean on every visit.

Every building is paired with a consistent team and a direct point of contact for property managers and Hausverwaltungen, with every visit logged and a 24‑hour return guarantee.
