Anya Trần

Brooklyn based musician and new media artist originally from Moscow with Vietnamese roots.

Steeped in a rigorous classical piano background, she shifted her focus to the theremin, embracing a direct, almost tactile encounter with sound that breaks free from traditional constraints.
